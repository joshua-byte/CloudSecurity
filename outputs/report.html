Method and interpretation

CloudPulse detects unusual event-count and failure-count activity using a rolling median and median absolute deviation (MAD), then evaluates selected CloudTrail security rules. Single-event rules can alert without a statistical spike because rare high-impact actions may not create a volume anomaly.

Confirmed means a rule match overlaps statistical evidence; it does not prove an attack. Rule-only means a rule matched without overlapping statistical evidence. Needs review indicates an unexplained statistical anomaly.

Limitations

  • Detection uses fixed thresholds and selected rules; it may miss activity outside their coverage.
  • Public or sample data may not represent a complete production audit trail.
  • Rule matches may reflect legitimate administrative activity and require analyst validation.
  • This version processes local files only; it does not ingest live AWS events or remediate findings.
